Software-Defined Radar

A project for a software-defined radio practicum -- the goal is to design a short-range distance-sensing Radar in GNU Radio on the Adalm Pluto SDR platform, but I am running into issues achieving enough chirp bandwidth given the Pluto's limited ADC sampling rate. Even if the project is not a success, I'm learning a lot about digital signal processing & FMCW Radar!

EMF Tracking Teleop Glove

What I REALLY want to do for my senior design project: Design a haptic glove that has a module mounted to it which generates a magnetic field; each joint has a magnet mounted to it, and based on the displacement of the magnetic field, the position of the user's hand can be mapped. This pairs with a simple robotic arm for teleoperation. A pipe dream for now, but not for long...
